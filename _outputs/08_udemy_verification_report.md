## Verification Protocol

For each Udemy course, the verifier must:

1. **Log in** to the organisation's Udemy account
2. **Navigate** to the course page using the provided URL
3. **Extract** and confirm:
   - Exact course title (as displayed on landing page)
   - Instructor name and credentials
   - Total duration (hours), lecture count, section count
   - Current rating (X.X / 5.0) and total number of ratings
   - Total enrollments (exact number)
   - Last updated date (month and year)
   - Full curriculum outline (section titles + lecture titles)
   - Language and subtitles
   - Current price (regular + sale)
4. **Cross-check** curriculum against track requirements (≥70% alignment required)
5. **Review** 5 most recent 1-star and 2-star reviews for red flags
6. **Preview** the course (audio quality, video quality, pacing)
7. **Document** findings in this report

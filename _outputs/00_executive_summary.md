## Programme Overview

This document presents a comprehensive AI upskilling programme designed to rapidly build AI literacy and applied AI skills across four professional roles. The programme is **100% self-paced**, **budget-friendly** (≥60% free content per track), and structured for enterprise-scale deployment.

### Four Parallel Learning Tracks

| Track | Target Audience | Focus | Duration | Cost/Learner |
|-------|----------------|-------|----------|-------------|
| 🟦 **Track A — Developers** | Frontend, backend, full-stack, mobile devs | Using AI coding tools (Copilot, Claude Code, Antigravity) as productivity multipliers | 16 weeks | ₹1,000–1,600 / $20–30 |
| 🟩 **Track B — QA Engineers** | Manual, automation, SDET engineers | Testing AI-powered features, validating LLM outputs, AI quality assurance | 12 weeks | ₹1,500–2,400 / $30–45 |
| 🟨 **Track C — Business Analysts** | BAs, product owners, requirements engineers | AI literacy, AI-ready requirements, feasibility assessment, governance | 10 weeks | ₹500–800 / $10–15 |
| 🟥 **Track D — AI/ML Developers** | Engineers building AI/ML systems | Training models, RAG, agents, MLOps, production AI systems | 16 weeks | ₹1,000–1,600 / $20–30 |

### Key Design Principles

- **Zero Course Overlap**: No course appears in more than one track. Shared topics (AI fundamentals, prompt engineering) use different, role-specific courses per track.
- **Track A ≠ Track D**: Developers *use* AI tools to code faster. AI/ML Developers *build* AI systems. Clear boundary enforced.
- **≥60% Free Content**: Every track exceeds the 60% free content threshold. Overall programme is ~78% free.
- **Self-Paced with Structure**: Suggested 4–6 hrs/week cadence with milestone checkpoints, but fully flexible.
- **Practical over Theoretical**: Hands-on projects, real codebases (Track A Tier 3B), and cross-functional capstones.

## Programme Structure

### Three Proficiency Tiers (+ Track A Bonus Tier)

| Tier | Focus | Assessment |
|------|-------|-----------|
| **Tier 1: Foundational** | Role-specific AI literacy and tool setup | Quiz (>80%) + 500-word reflection |
| **Tier 2: Intermediate** | Applied AI skills in daily workflows | Mini-project + peer review |
| **Tier 3: Advanced** | Strategic AI practices and cross-functional collaboration | Cross-functional capstone (4-role team) |
| **Tier 3B: Brownfield** (Track A only) | AI-assisted legacy code modernisation | Real legacy module case study |

### Cross-Functional Capstone
The Tier 3 capstone brings together one person from each track to collaborate on a real AI initiative:
- **BA** writes the business case and requirements
- **AI Dev** architects and builds the AI component
- **Developer** integrates using AI coding tools
- **QA** validates with AI-specific testing strategies

# Learning Track A — Developers (Agentic AI-Assisted Development)

> **Track Colour**: 🟦 Blue  
> **Target Audience**: Software developers (frontend, backend, full-stack, mobile) who will use AI-powered coding tools to accelerate their daily development workflow.  
> **Focus**: Tool mastery, NOT theory. Using AI agents and copilots as force multipliers.  
> **Duration**: 16 weeks (4–6 hrs/week) | **Total Hours**: ~80 hours  
> **Free Content**: ~65%

---

## Track Overview

```
Week 1–3   → Tier 1: AI Foundations & Tool Setup (12–18 hrs)
Week 4–8   → Tier 2: AI-Augmented Development Workflows (20–30 hrs)
Week 9–12  → Tier 3: Advanced Agentic Development & Team Practices (16–24 hrs)
Week 13–16 → Tier 3B: Brownfield & Legacy Modernisation with AI (16–24 hrs)
```

> **Navigation**: Experienced developers who already use Copilot may skip to Tier 2. Take the Tier 1 checkpoint quiz — score >80% to skip.

  5. Generate documentation using AI

# Maintenance Plan

> **Purpose**: Quarterly review and refresh schedule to keep the AI upskilling programme current.  
> **Principle**: AI moves fast — stale training content does more harm than good.

---

## Quarterly Review Cycle

### Q1 (January–March): Full Link Audit + Udemy Re-Verification

| Action | Details | Owner | Time Estimate |
|--------|---------|-------|---------------|
| **Link Audit** | Click every course URL across all 4 tracks. Flag broken/removed links. | L&D Admin | 2 hours |
| **Udemy Re-Verification** | Log in to Udemy, check all 8 courses: still exist, ratings stable, content updated, no stale reviews | L&D Team | 4 hours |
| **View/Enrollment Update** | Update view counts (YouTube) and enrollment counts (Udemy/Coursera) in course cards | L&D Admin | 2 hours |
| **Freshness Check** | Flag any course with "Last Updated" > 12 months ago | L&D Admin | 1 hour |
| **Learner Feedback Review** | Analyse Q4 feedback surveys for content quality issues | L&D Team | 2 hours |
| **Action Items** | Replace broken/stale courses. Update course cards. Document changes. | L&D Team | 4 hours |

**Deliverable**: Updated course list + change log

---

### Q2 (April–June): New Course Integration

| Action | Details | Owner | Time Estimate |
|--------|---------|-------|---------------|

| **Overlap Check** | Verify any new course doesn't duplicate existing track content | L&D Team | 2 hours |

| **Curriculum Update** | Integrate approved new courses. Update track documents. | L&D Team | 3 hours |
| **View/Enrollment Update** | Refresh all view/enrollment counts | L&D Admin | 2 hours |
| **Track A vs D Audit** | Re-run boundary analysis for any new courses | L&D Team | 1 hour |

**Deliverable**: Updated track documents + pilot test results

## Course Health Monitoring

### Red Flag Indicators (Immediate Action Required)

| Indicator | Threshold | Action |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Course removed from platform | N/A | Replace within 1 week |
| Rating dropped below 4.0 | <4.0 / 5 | Investigate + find alternative |
| >20% of recent reviews say "outdated" | Manual review | Replace within 2 weeks |
| YouTube video made private/deleted | N/A | Replace within 1 week |
| Instructor no longer active | No updates in 12+ months | Monitor; replace if content stales |

### Yellow Flag Indicators (Monitor + Plan)

| Indicator | Threshold | Action |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| Last updated >12 months ago | Date check | Flag for replacement in next quarterly review |
| Enrollment/views stagnating | <1% growth per quarter | Consider if newer alternative exists |
| Mixed learner feedback | NPS 7.0–7.5 | Investigate specific complaints |
| New competitor course released | Ongoing scan | Evaluate and potentially swap |
